# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro 5 (static pages + one serverless API route) on Vercel, Supabase Postgres for leads, Three.js for the hero object. Chosen for near-zero JS outside the 3D scene, HTML-first authoring, and one-click Vercel deploys.

## Users

Founders, owners and marketing leads of cafés, restaurants, events, startups, D2C and service brands in India and internationally. They are evaluating whether to hand their social media, content, ads and lead handling to one outside team, usually on a phone, often arriving from Instagram.

## Product Purpose

Zannid & Co. is a strategy-led growth agency. The website exists to turn a visitor into a booked free growth audit (form or WhatsApp). Success = qualified audit requests.

## Positioning

One team owns the whole growth engine (strategy, reels/shoots, editing, design, Meta ads, creators, websites, AI DM/WhatsApp/call automation) and is measured on growth, not post counts. Tagline system: "Strategy. Content. Growth." / "Think. Create. Scale." / "Ideas that scale."

## Operating Context

Primary acquisition is Instagram (@zannid.co, DM keyword "GROW"). Leads are followed up on WhatsApp. Works with clients nationally and internationally; bills INR or USD.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services: social media management, reel shoots, product/food shoots, video editing, graphic design, Meta ads, influencer marketing, website development, AI automations. Monthly plans, no lock-in; ad spend billed separately. Current offer: free growth audit + 30-day plan (user-set value ₹4,999, limited monthly slots). Prices are user-approved defaults and editable in one data file. Contact WhatsApp and email are placeholders until the user supplies them.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Zannid & Co."; wide Syncopate wordmark with "& CO." set right; iridescent glass blob with a black orbit ring and a "#" mark (from the brand's Instagram identity); italic blade-cut Z monogram; achromatic black/white UI where colour lives only in the nucleus and photography; Gen-Z bold voice, Hinglish welcome in content.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: three reels on Instagram (Biryani "dum nahi diye", Café "Konsi café jaenge", launch "Hello" film) with their public engagement; brand carousel copy. Absent: client testimonials, client logos, verified client results. Do not fabricate these; results are presented as plan targets and sourced industry statistics, editable in src/data/site.ts.

## Product Principles

- One action: every screen leads to the free growth audit.
- Prove, don't claim: show the work, the process and sourced facts.
- One owner: the site itself should feel like one team's hand, one theme.
- Respect the scroll: motion guides (the ring), it never blocks reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard access, reduced-motion support (3D and ring rotation calm down), labelled form fields.
